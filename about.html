03 — About

A bit about me

I'm Denin — a mechanical design engineer by trade, based in Scarborough, Ontario. I'm a Certified SolidWorks Professional, which mostly means I can't help but model things I probably don't need to.

Outside of work, I split my free time between two things that scratch the same itch: photography and personal engineering projects. One is about noticing light and moments, the other about noticing how things fit together — both are really just an excuse to keep making.

This site is where I put both, with no particular schedule. New stuff shows up whenever I get around to it.
